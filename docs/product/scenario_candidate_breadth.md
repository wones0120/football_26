# Frozen scenario candidate breadth research

In **Analyst Reports → Frozen scenario candidate research replay**, enter a saved Classic `SCENARIO_FIRST` optimizer run ID and choose **Build frozen candidate comparison**. Reopen its immutable result with **Saved research comparisons**. This public research action intentionally supports locked historical slates without invoking operational Optimize, writing lineup records, changing user settings, clearing entry eligibility, or creating a DraftKings export. The original saved run remains unchanged.

The original generator allocated one idea per requested slot and constructed one roster before moving on. Its persisted candidates could therefore equal the selected portfolio, despite more supported ideas existing. The research policy attempts two legal alternatives for every saved evidence-qualified idea. The second response combines a sourced supporting idea from a different game; moments for disjoint games compose without applying two opportunity changes to one game. A no-good cut requests a distinct complete roster, not exclusion of a named player. Both opportunity budgets and solver failures remain visible. User preferences and base model moments are unchanged.

The candidate objective remains conditional mean plus 0.35 of the sum of marginal P90 spreads. This is a sensitivity score, **not joint lineup P90 or calibrated tournament upside**. Existing conserved opportunity design and elasticity remain unvalidated response assumptions.

The pool includes the original selected rosters as explicitly identified controls. Candidate variants remain distinct evidence; canonical roster identity deduplicates portfolio entries. Selection enforces the ordinary exposure/overlap/game constraints and the original allocation pair/triple/discovery controls. Research selection uses existing ordinal evidence/capability priorities in conditional coverage. These are design priorities, not event probabilities. **Highest baseline mean control on same candidate pool** preserves a separate expected-score objective. More candidates can help measure coverage; they do not establish predictive improvement.

The policy remains experimental and is not a changed live default. Frozen replay keeps original snapshot/packet/settings/config hashes, source run ID and implementation hash. Historical outcomes may already have been inspected: no outcome data enters the API's generation function, and freezing now does not create held-out validation. Stored source timestamps do not certify exact original source availability or reproduce original code. Future promotion needs chronological evidence and football review across independent slates, not a favorable known Week6 outcome.

Public API: POST `/api/learning/research/scenario-breadth/{optimizer_run_id}`; GET `/api/learning/research/scenario-breadth`; GET `/api/learning/research/scenario-breadth/saved/{replay_id}`. Artifacts are immutable under `artifacts/scenario_breadth_replays`; equivalent source/config/implementation returns the original ID. Corrections create a new version through a changed implementation/source hash. No new configuration keys or dependencies.
