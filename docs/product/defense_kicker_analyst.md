# Defense and kicker Analyst passes

`defense_kicker_analyst.scan_specialists(packet, rows, fmt=...)` screens every confirmed game and canonical DST/K row. Classic screens DST only. Showdown screens optional defense and kicker inclusion, usually FLEX; no Captain ban or mandatory specialist selection is inferred. Base projections and user preferences stay separate.

Optional packets use `defense_kicker_analysis_v1` under `defense_kicker_analysis`. Findings retain canonical/native identity, format, game, source clocks, expiry, mechanism, contrary evidence, uncertainty, model accounting, recheck triggers and lineup opportunity cost. Every specialty dimension has reviewed/unavailable status. Reviewed dimensions require exact assertions in verified captured sources. Unavailable numerical evidence stays null. Unknown inputs produce watchlists; scoring bonuses, probabilities and selection weights are prohibited.

Defense dimensions cover opponent QB/protection, actual pressure, dropback/pace opportunity, turnover variance, points-allowed scoring, starters/injuries and special teams. Sacks are pressure conversions; turnovers and defensive TDs have high variance. Low opponent totals or favoritism alone are insufficient. Own RB/game-control and opposing QB/receiver correlations remain conditional; existing avoid-DST-opponents controls remain authoritative.

Kicker dimensions cover confirmed role, drives/scoring chances, red-zone stalls versus TD/XP outcomes, distance/scoring rules, sourced weather/venue, fourth-down tendencies and accuracy uncertainty. Missing weather cannot mean calm weather. Strong offense alone does not establish field-goal ceiling. A scoring definition must be verified before a new numerical estimate; this version retains baseline forecasts.

`paired_flex_comparison(snapshot, specialist_id=..., captain_id=...)` creates included/omitted alternatives under the identical captured legal model, objective and Captain. It reports canonical rosters, salary, baseline mean, displaced players, solver status and requested/achieved counts. Locks, exposure gates and model constraints survive. Infeasible branches remain explicit with no relaxation. This is a comparison API, not an automatic exposure allocator. Supported-comparison findings require reviewed core opportunity/role dimensions and distinct partial/unmodeled accounting.

The [Wednesday edition](../../research/defense_kicker_analysts_20261014/v2/REVIEW.md) contains twenty Sunday Main DST screens and eight actual SEA–DEN same-Captain paired comparisons. All current specialist findings are watchlists because measured pressure/protection/drives/stall evidence is missing. Thursday inputs preserve Tuesday source/model clocks; the edition retains its actual generation clock. SNF DAL–GB and MNF WAS–SF remain schedule-level research without salary scope. No live mutation or entry-ready recommendation is produced.

Every future Analyst briefing should rerun both passes after canonical scope/role refresh, disclose dimension coverage and source clocks, and record what changes the prior view. Use current primary sources for injuries/role/weather; immutable captures and lineage are required. Previously inspected kicker/defense results remain retrospective evidence, not fresh held-out validation.
