# Offensive workload comparison

## Role-aware efficiency and projected yards — experimental Showdown comparison

Choose **Optimizer comparison → Learned opportunities → projected yards · experimental**, with scenario mode off, then use the ordinary **Prepare slate for optimization → Run Optimizer → Reopen results** workflow. This is a separate objective (`learned_yards`); original `learned_counts`, reference and points comparisons remain available. Changing between count/yard modes requires the corresponding frozen Prepare receipt. The server rejects receipt substitution or altered weights. This delivery validates the feature and forecasts without generating another operational Thursday lineup or CSV.

The exact selection formula is **0.04 × projected passing yards + 0.10 × projected rushing yards + 0.10 × projected receiving yards**. These are the existing application’s DraftKings yardage scoring components, fixed policy weights, not fitted optimal weights. There is no positional count normalization, reception, touchdown, interception, bonus, ownership or full fantasy-point/P90 contribution. Captain utility/salary multiply by 1.5; physical counts/yards do not. QB receiving counts are unsupported and remain outside individual QB utility; their targets are retained in the team residual. Season-average points-leader preferences are inactive for this comparison request with original values/reason recorded; stored settings remain unchanged. Factual eligibility and user roster/salary/exposure/QB constraints still apply.

Efficiency uses matched yard/count numerator-denominator pairs: yards per pass attempt, rush attempt, or target. Yards per target includes incompletions; do not multiply catch rate again. Negative observed yard outcomes remain signed. Each canonical player’s last eight observed same-team, same-position games in the current season are pooled, then shrunk toward a prior position/usage-role cohort with fixed prior-equivalent opportunities: passing 80, rushing 30, receiving 20. A zero known count produces zero yards without inventing a rate; missing counts never become zero. No complete supported rate means unknown forecast/selection ineligibility. A new player can receive an explicitly population-only estimate with zero player-history rows; this does not invent participation evidence or override a missing count forecast.

Usage role means the mean of the last three observed same-team/position/season games: QB passes ≥20, RB carries ≥10, WR/TE targets ≥5 identify higher usage; lower observed usage identifies rotational usage. Missing history yields unknown role. Historical cohort roles use only observations earlier than each cohort game. These are declared descriptive policy thresholds, not canonical depth-chart roles or tuned winner rules. Cohorts need ten matched rows and 40 opportunities; sparse role cohorts fall back explicitly to the prior position population with the same minimum support. Current efficiency is conditional on role persistence and active participation. No opponent, pass depth/YAC or designed-run/scramble/kneel separation is modeled.

Team passing yards sum supported QB forecasts plus an explicitly unallocated pass-count residual at a prior QB population rate. Raw receiving forecasts plus unallocated targets at a prior receiving population rate are scaled by one reported team reconciliation factor to that physical passing total. This adjustment can increase or decrease receiving yards; it changes no counts and donates no unknown opportunities to known players. Missing/invalid budgets fail; unsupported reconciliation leaves receiving forecasts unknown. Factors outside 2/3–1.5 carry a sensitivity warning, without silently clipping forecasts. The same passing/receiving event can legitimately contribute to both QB and receiver fantasy utility; it is one physical event in team accounting.

Prepare shows projected yards, prior rates, sample opportunities/source references, shrinkage/role assumptions, exact weights, team residuals and reconciliation factors. The full frozen receipt retains original count receipt SHA, source IDs/history SHA, policy/code hashes and training period. Status responses omit full history ID lists; saved audits retain them. Original count utility stays in each actor as a comparison. Solver audits retain declared weights and distinguish partial DK yard scoring from the unused full fantasy projection.

Every yard Prepare runs a fixed six-prior-period chronological **conditional efficiency** diagnostic against a prior position-average baseline. Actual attempts/targets are supplied only for this diagnostic; the reported error is not end-to-end learned-count/yard accuracy, and current team reconciliation is checked separately. Revised imports and previously inspected outcomes do not establish original pregame availability or fresh held-out evidence. Current results are mixed: passing/rushing errors improve slightly; receiving error worsens slightly. No search/tuning, calibrated uncertainty, tournament win probability or lineup superiority is claimed. The comparison remains experimental; it does not replace the standard optimizer. ## Latest learned opportunities — Thursday Showdown

Choose **Optimizer comparison → Learned opportunities · no points selection gates**, leave scenario mode off, then use **Prepare slate for optimization → Run Optimizer → Reopen results**. Missing required learned counts stay unknown; there is no reference-share or incumbent-points fallback. The original points lineup and old optimizer remain separate saved comparisons.

## Reference workload and FP control paths


In Captain Operations, **Optimizer comparison → Reference offensive workload forecasts**, then **Prepare slate for optimization** creates an immutable count-forecast receipt. Run Optimizer consumes that receipt; reopen the saved run to inspect physical forecasts and original fantasy-point projections. Standard remains the default and existing saved runs are preserved. Classic inputs are supported by the shared producer, but this first operational optimizer slice supports Captain only.

The reference producer issues next-game team pass/rush forecasts with fixed Ridge alpha30 from prior team-game lag3/8 volumes. It forecasts player counts from those future budgets and a fixed half latest/half last3 same-team opportunity share. Full recent team-game coverage and a sourced stable starter are required for QB passing. It does not relabel `targets_mean_3` as a forecast, import research training runners, fabricate injury replacement shares or change fantasy-point projections. The saved research trees are not served because their complete current feature contract has not been implemented.

All source actors and current native salary actors enter before user exclusions. Targets fit within passes; only downward reconciliation is permitted. Unknown or excluded opportunities are not donated to eligible players. Missing participant rows remain unknown, not observed zero. Forecasts are conditional on active participation and stable observed role; the model does not infer that a newly available player automatically resumes a former role. Current source clocks, canonical IDs, raw IDs, trained coefficients/features, code hash and receipt hash are retained.

Newly prepared forecasts also freeze `history_evidence` under `opportunity_history_evidence_v1`. It retains original per-game counts, native/source IDs and import clocks, selected same-team role windows and primary inactive-gap proofs, and full-source team component/missing/unmapped coverage. Allocated proxies and forward estimates never become original observations. A source stat row alone does not certify participation. Supplied future imports, invalid counts and duplicate native/canonical player-game rows are rejected. Publication, capture or exact event clocks absent from these inputs remain explicitly unknown; revised history is not original pregame proof. This shared adapter extraction preserves the existing numeric count policy and defaults. Its code/test review does not itself establish that an already running service loaded it.

The descriptive `offensive_opportunity_events_v1` adapter partitions known pass/rush/target events into outside20, 6–20, inside5 and unknown field position; goal-to-go is reported separately. Sacks, spikes, kneels, scrambles, two-point tries and no-plays remain distinct, and missing eligibility/receiver attribution produces incomplete totals rather than false zero counts. These are descriptive event counts, not new zone/TD forecasts. Count receipts can retain a supplied, cutoff-bound event receipt. Normal Prepare currently records event capture as `unavailable`: the operational immutable-PBP intake/actual SourceSnapshot artifact-verification hook remains pending. No mutable research source is loaded into serving, and no zone feature or forecast is claimed active from this adapter alone.

Utility is the average of equally weighted normalized applicable count components:

- QB: pass attempts / prior-training QB pass mean, and rush attempts / prior-training QB rush mean.
- RB/WR/TE: targets / prior-training position target mean, and rush attempts / prior-training position rush mean.
- Divisors have floor1. These preference weights are transparent, not equivalent fantasy-point or scoring opportunities. QB passes and receiving targets can describe the same physical event.

Salary is only a cap/cost constraint. FP, P90, ownership and story bonuses do not enter count utility. Captain1.5 multiplies utility and salary, never physical forecast counts. Existing Captain qualification and user restrictions remain in force. Sequential solves use remaining user exposure limits and preserve50%Captain when configured; infeasibility is reported without relaxing restrictions. Ten-second single-thread limits bound each solve.

K/DST are explicitly outside the offensive model. Unknown required counts cannot receive a fake zero utility. Their original model assessment remains available in the standard run. **Comparison · FP mean on same offensive domain** isolates the objective change on the same supported domain; the ordinary all-domain standard is a separate comparison. Neither opt-in mode replaces the standard recommendation.

Chronological revised-data W2–5 checks use a model frozen beforeW2, with already inspected outcomes and a conditional participation cohort. Errors are mixed by component/position; they do not establish superiority, count calibration, contest value or injury-response quality. Missing actual participants are reported separately. See [frozen design](../../research/workload_count_optimizer_20261019/v1/PROTOCOL.md). High review and actual public Prepare→Optimize→reopen→CSV acceptance are required before an operational acceptance claim. Downloading does not enter a contest.
