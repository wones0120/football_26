# Canonical opportunity source evidence

The operational PBP boundary captures the full installed provider return through `SourceCaptureService` before player/team filtering. Its immutable CSV, manifest and indexed `SourceSnapshot` retain the source revision and availability clocks. Provider cache freshness, original historical publication and complete-game coverage remain explicit unknowns. A capture clock is not evidence of original pregame availability.

`derive_opportunity_event_evidence` verifies the raw artifact and manifest, then selects prior regular-season events. Native game/play keys, canonical teams and GSIS-to-UUID aliases bind identity. Recognized administrative controls remain retained separately; an unrecognized record stays unknown. Missing mappings produce identity-review requests rather than name joins or silent drops. Official pass/rush/target views are linked events, not additive offensive budgets. Exclusive outside20/6–20/inside5/unknown zones and overlapping goal-to-go retain channel coverage and lower bounds.

`freeze_event_artifact` writes a deterministic bounded gzip artifact. `load_event_artifact` checks its compressed/expanded hashes, reference fields and the actual indexed raw snapshot, without fetching data. `semantic_event_view` attaches selected actors to full-team budgets and preserves unallocated residuals. Missing actors are listed explicitly; they do not receive zero counts. Actor attachment and user exclusions do not redistribute opportunities.

For serving, `freeze_scalar_artifact` first loads and verifies that full parent, computes the selected scalar view itself and writes a separate immutable JSON artifact. `load_scalar_artifact` bounds and checks the smaller file, its publication/scope/reference fields, the unchanged parent compressed-byte hash and actual indexed raw snapshot. It does not inflate the full parent on each Prepare. Global parent and selected-team semantic hashes have distinct names. Durable server-owned discovery and the shared Prepare hook are integration responsibilities; an arbitrary caller-authored view or research-path fallback is not an accepted source.

The semantic measurement hash covers definitions, canonical facts, prior event revisions, missingness, reconciliation and source-quality states. Canonical ordering makes equivalent row order and recapture envelopes stable. Source artifact hashes, capture/execution clocks and original record order remain provenance. A changed count, attribution, prior measurement or quality state remains significant.

The [actual source and compact handoff receipts](../../research/opportunity_source_capture_20261019/actual/compact_handoff_receipt.json) verify one captured source and its smaller WAS/SF actor view. This is descriptive measured evidence, with `forecast=None`; it does not add a fantasy-point/count bonus or establish a zone/TD forecast. The server-owned publication and Prepare/Analyst consumer are implemented; actual new-run acceptance is tracked separately from descriptive artifact verification. ## Numeric team counts and conditional scoring

An append-only source publication verifies the indexed raw snapshot and managed scalar/defense/environment artifacts. A separate model publication accepts only a managed fit result and verified tree/neural artifacts. Prepare performs fresh inference without fitting. Training-matrix identity and fresh ordered input identity remain separate. Repeated quote capture with unchanged values does not change numerical dependencies; actual line, count, role, component or model-contract changes do.

The first team models use 57 ordered raw inputs (114 after train-only preprocessing/missing indicators): four prior pass/rush lags, pregame total and team spread, observed snap/window sizes, and 12 own/opponent environment/defense variants with exposure and missingness. They are not 57 completed catalog candidates. Both fixed families predict the same official pass/rush targets; the declared arithmetic mean retains both values and their disagreement. Disagreement is not a calibrated interval. Historical 2023–25 measurements and closing lines are revised later captures; original historical decision availability remains unknown. Current quotes require indexed provenance and prelock clocks.

Player forecasts retain full-source budgets, prior conditional role shares and unallocated work. Only a matching typed factual unavailable proof creates an injury vacancy. Its hypothetical active-role reference is separate from the actual inactive zero-production world. Recipient fractions require sourced current roles and explicit authored assumptions; they are not estimated recipient shares or probabilities. Missing reference quantities remain unknown, and user exclusions do not donate opportunities.

For this branch, conditional FP is frozen base FP plus the new-minus-reference change in supported linear scoring components: passing yards/interceptions, rushing yards, receptions and receiving yards. Rates use matched prior numerator/denominator samples. QB/receiver yards and catches share team budgets, with downward-only reconciliation and residuals. TDs, bonuses, fumbles and other undecomposed terms remain unchanged for active actors; this is a declared reference-count anchoring approximation, not a decomposition of the total-FP model. Negative anchoring diagnostics and unsupported components are visible. Factual unavailable actors have zero complete-world production, with actual FP delta distinguished from the linear-only delta.

Authored scenarios transform physical count budgets once, then apply this measured component difference. Legacy total-FP elasticity is explicitly unconsumed in the new branch. Captain multiplies scoring once. The frozen prepared receipt retains full efficiency/source/model proofs; affected-game research requests carry semantic hashes and thin native-player changes. The saved app/report displays base versus conditional FP, supported components, role assumptions, alternatives and shared risks.

Code acceptance and actual source/model fit receipts are in `research/opportunity_component_response_20261019/v1` and `research/team_opportunity_forecast_20261019/v1`. New public Prepare→research closure→Optimize→saved reopen/native CSV acceptance must be recorded before calling a new lineup ready; earlier saved lineup receipts are not relabeled.
