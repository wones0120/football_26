# Complete-lineup top-decile model research

The bounded prototype in `research/top_decile_lineup_model_20261018/v1` learns from top-decile versus remaining complete lineups, separately for Classic and Showdown. The app's **Analyst Reports** catalog exposes **Complete-lineup top-decile model — prototype and coverage limits**. Reading this report does not activate a selector or modify player projections.

Labels are contest-local: score at `ceil(0.10 × valid final field entries)`, including ties after normalizing DraftKings CSV float noise to cent precision. Canonical roster identity includes Showdown Captain role. Native contest/slot/salary provenance is required for identity, and exact stored prelock forecast/feature clocks are checked. Missing projections are excluded, not imputed. Tiers and Single Stat are inventoried separately and never treated as Classic labels.

Training uses a label-independent deterministic sample of at most5,000 canonical rosters per contest. Full-field labels and feature coverage remain visible. Duplicate roster entries/contests retain multiplicity/provenance; one canonical roster receives one total training loss weight across input variants, with fractional Bernoulli labels preserving different contest-boundary membership. Multiplicity and postgame outcomes are not predictors.

The fixed-C regularized logistic models use whole-week chronological folds and training-only scalers. Core features describe the complete roster's projected strength, uncertainty summary, salary and team/game dependencies; rich adds recorded pregame workload and game context. `marginal_p90_spread_sum` is not joint lineupP90. Scores are unvalidated context-specific top-decile propensities, not contest-win or cash probabilities. Original source/code chronology is incompletely certified, and previously inspected2026outcomes remain retrospective despite temporal splits. Few independent slates prevent confident promotion.

The saved38-roster frozen-pool comparison keeps original settings, old strategy and highest-mean controls. A separately named external complete-lineup utility is passed to the research selector; player projection values are never overwritten. On the current retrospective Main pool no candidate reaches the observed field decile cutoff, demonstrating a candidate-coverage ceiling rather than a selector success.

No live default or operational lineup behavior is changed. Coefficients/scalers are portable JSON, with model/data/source hashes, per-contest metrics, calibration references, exclusions and full selected rosters. Reproduction scripts are explicitly research workflows; they do not establish original source chronology or fresh held-out validation. Future operational generation still uses the normal public Prepare→Optimize workflow.
