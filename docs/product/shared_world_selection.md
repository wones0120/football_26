# Expected anchor and shared conditional coverage

Captain GPP offers **Portfolio selection policy → Expected anchor + shared conditional coverage** alongside the existing intent selector. The choice is stored per context. It requires automatic current authored research with a verified prepared producer closure and uses the common checked parent packet. Explicit personal what-ifs cannot become automatic contrary worlds. Existing modes keep their behavior.

The first roster maximizes conditional mean points in the unique supported authored Expected Game world, subject to completion of the requested portfolio. If the authored anchor is ambiguous, the receipt explicitly uses unchanged base-model means and records why. Further entries add the largest positive conditional point gain over the best already selected roster in any supported common world. All candidates are evaluated against the same expected and contrary worlds. Equal numeric world columns retain their original source aliases without gaining duplicate influence.

This policy has no mandatory strategy-owned QB/receiver stacks or starting-QB rule. Actual Captain workload eligibility, Q policy, locks/exclusions, salary, team/game/QB limits, leader settings, exposures and overlap remain binding. Omitted strategy predicates and retained controls are saved separately. User exclusions never change forecasts or donate opportunities.

Conditional means score each physical player once, with Captain points multiplied once by1.5. Counts, ownership and Analyst judgments are not extra point bonuses. Missing P90 does not exclude a player with a valid mean; the tail remains unknown. No sum of marginal player P90 is presented as lineup P90. World likelihood, joint tail, win probability and payout EV are unknown. An extreme conditional world can influence an additional entry; its mechanism, assumptions and unknown likelihood remain visible. If no positive coverage gain exists, the report says so and chooses the strongest remaining feasible anchor roster.

Generation searches each common world before spending additional work on Captain families and variants. It is bounded at96 candidates/96 solver calls/60 seconds, with at most two seconds per solve. Selection uses a ten-second exact hard-model completion oracle. A timeout is not infeasibility, and failure of the bounded candidate pool does not prove the original full legal model cannot complete. No underfilled prefix or hidden constraint relaxation is accepted as the requested portfolio.

The saved-results section **Whole-lineup game-world selection** and readable report show each full roster, target authored mechanism, conditional scores/gain, source/counterevidence/unknowns, captured role and inference tier, shared actors/team opportunities, and the strongest admissible full-roster alternative with entering/leaving players and salary/point tradeoffs. Recommendation and native CSV export preserve the frozen anchor-first order rather than reranking by base FP or P90. Reopening reads saved evidence.
