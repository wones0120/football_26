# Experimental optimizer scenario mode

In **Operations → Prepare slate**, choose Classic or Captain and enable **Compare one scenario lineup with a matched baseline lineup**. This opt-in choice starts off and does not replace your saved optimizer settings. It supports Classic Large GPP and Captain GPP with one lineup; multi-lineup portfolios and player-lock scenarios remain outside this version.

Choose one slate team, set passing/rushing volume factors between 0.5 and 1.5, and describe your assumption. The default enabled scoring mode is **New models drive selection · complete score heads**; factors of one keep the new forecast unchanged. The separate **Legacy anchored sensitivity · comparison only** mode requires a changed factor. Select **Prepare slate for optimization**, inspect **Prepared scenario**, then **Run Optimizer**. Editing an assumption or turning the mode off requires Prepare again. Stored player locks are retained and must be cleared explicitly to use the one-lineup comparison. Existing salary, eligibility, team/game, exposure, DST-opponent and single-TE controls continue through the normal workflow.

Primary mode requires published team and player tree/neural pairs and a current verified DST expert. Their opportunity estimates feed a separately fitted, prior-only complete DraftKings score head for each offensive position and kicker. The target includes touchdowns, bonuses, returns, conversions and lost fumbles; kicker scoring includes distance and extra points. Incumbent FP predictions are absent from offensive and kicker inference. Conditional scoring-head validation uses historical observed counts and is **not** end-to-end forecast validation. Count estimates are transformed-scale point approximations, without proven arithmetic-mean calibration.

Primary Captain selection uses complete new scores for CPT (×1.5) and FLEX. All score-supported actors remain candidates under factual and user hard rules; the incumbent P90 shortlist does not constrain this arm. Missing complete scores remain visible and are ineligible, with no old-projection fallback. The saved old control retains Captain P90/FLEX mean, its original shortlist and full eligible pool. Thus this is a forecast and generation-policy comparison, not a controlled selector-only experiment. Each solve remains capped at ten seconds. No superiority, win probability or full game-clock simulation is claimed.

Prepare retains the normal baseline projection run and separately freezes a scenario receipt with its canonical actor identities, counts/component ancestry, configuration, cutoff, source parent, shared budgets, supported component changes and missing evidence. Scenario changes do not overwrite stored player fantasy projections or ownership. Passing volume scales team passing attempts and target opportunities together; rushing volume scales carries. Full-source unknown/unassigned opportunities remain explicit rather than being redistributed over a salary subset.

The legacy sensitivity scoring approximation reuses matched prior non-TD linear DraftKings components: passing yards/interceptions, rushing yards, receptions and receiving yards. Receiving yards/catches remain within shared passing/catch budgets. Unsupported components retain baseline values and are listed per player. Touchdowns, bonuses and the undecomposed baseline remainder stay fixed. Baseline/component anchor inconsistencies are disclosed. If a workload reference cannot be produced, the receipt reports **Missing workload evidence; baseline retained**, lists the reason and shows zero supported changes. The optimizer can still produce a clearly labeled baseline-equivalent comparison; it does not invent replacement inputs.

Legacy sensitivity optimization performs at most two CBC solves, each capped at ten seconds: the highest baseline-mean lineup and the conditional-mean lineup over the same eligible pool and supported hard rules. The original optimizer remains available with scenario mode off. This matched baseline is an exact mean control, not a rerun of the existing multi-lineup GPP portfolio policy. No player lock, required QB stack, ownership bonus or scenario probability is introduced by this mode. Infeasibility remains a failure, not a constraint relaxation.

For legacy sensitivity Captain, the same prepared player means feed one CPT and five FLEX slots. Captain scores and salary are multiplied by 1.5; physical counts are not. Both solves retain the same baseline P90 Captain shortlist, player/team rules, QB/receiver rules and user preferences. The baseline is the highest mean within that matched eligibility policy, rather than the default Captain-P90 objective. Unknown K/DST/TD/bonus responses stay at baseline.

Saved results show both lineups' baseline scores, the scenario lineup's conditional score, the authored assumptions and player-level evidence gaps. The baseline lineup, solver statuses, scoring receipt and model snapshots are retained in the saved run, and reopening uses those records rather than current controls. The generated lineup's `projection` remains its baseline model mean; `scenario_mean` and `scenario_mode_player` retain conditional scores separately. Normal native CSV export applies to the selected saved lineup and retains existing upload/readiness checks.

These are authored conditional sensitivities with unknown likelihood. The mode does not implement the research game's full clock/continuation simulator, calibrated event assignment or validated replacement workloads. A higher conditional objective is not evidence of better win probability or predictive accuracy. Normal source/readiness and lock checks remain in force; this feature does not bypass them.
