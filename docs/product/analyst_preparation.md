# Automatic Analyst research in Prepare Slate

Leave **Prepared Analyst handoff** in automatic mode, then click **Prepare slate for optimization**. Preparation selects the newest usable published research matching season, week, format, exact canonical game teams and kickoff times. It compares fresh canonical roles/availability, salaries and declared workload/market baseline values, and saves a separate `analyst_preparation_v1` compatibility report and immutable checked packet. **Run Optimizer** receives that exact packet automatically; no packet selection or hash copy is required.

Original research prose, decision clocks, sources and artifacts are preserved. The check clock identifies when fresh prepared data was compared, not when new semantic research occurred. Explicit required-active-player contradictions, changed identity-specific workload allocation and expired actions are suppressed with reasons. A player mentioned in a hypothesis can represent an absence or counterevidence, so mere canonical linkage does not imply an active-role requirement. Fresh unknowns remain explicit. Machine checks cannot verify coaching intent, matchup narratives, psychology or forward calibration.

The receipt reports the source and checked packet hashes, original research version/date, check date, available packet features, changes, missing checks and considered/rejected candidates. `needs_review` with a checked packet permits disclosed experimental use; `needs_review` without a packet or `unavailable` requires new research or an explicit baseline-only choice. Normal source readiness and final entry clearance remain independent and are never weakened.

For Classic, automatic mixed merged research enables the existing discovery, challenger and teammate passes unless explicitly disabled. Selected-only focus preserves its declared branch and cannot introduce unrelated automatic alternatives. Explicit strategy/focus selections, locks, exclusions and exposure controls remain authoritative. A manual packet differing from the prepared source/checked packet requires preparing with that override. Baseline-only is an explicit choice; cash optimization requires this choice if the available packet uses merged GPP scenarios. No silent construction or baseline fallback occurs.

If normal Sunday source readiness is blocked by missing/stale injury or role evidence, open **Preliminary Classic research generation** and click **Prepare preliminary research inputs**. This explicitly refreshes markets, builds new research projections and ownership, excludes unknown availability/role players from scoring without recording them as medically unavailable, and automatically freezes a new immutable research snapshot. The visible assumption table lists each canonical player, unchanged source assessment, missing checks and exclusion decision. Review that table, choose the request-only rank restriction option if historical coverage is incomplete, accept **Use the displayed preliminary assumptions; keep final entry readiness false**, then click **Generate preliminary research lineups**. Saved normal-run preferences remain binding apart from explicit request-only choices. Identity, market, projection and ownership failures still block research; no final-ready override is available. A completed preliminary preparation cannot be used by the normal optimizer. Existing projection availability overrides must be explicitly cleared before preliminary preparation; they are never silently discarded. Older finished normal preparations may still be frozen if their non-injury inputs remain verified. Freeze saves an immutable matching Analyst sidecar and returns its checked packet automatically. Prior discovery actions tied to a different pool become watchlists pending semantic re-review, while the deterministic screen uses the new frozen pool. The separate snapshot/hash and compatibility artifact avoid circular or retroactive lineage. Preliminary runs stay not entry cleared and cannot export contest CSVs. Old snapshots without a checked sidecar remain explicit legacy artifacts; a new freeze is required for automatic use.

The durable optimizer request and saved result retain `analyst_handoff_applied`, the exact preparation/checked packet and compatibility hashes, and `football_analyst.prepared_research` / `preparation_compatibility`. Changed preparations or explicit hashes cannot silently reuse a different semantic receipt. Ordinary queue request idempotency remains payload-aware. Only immutable imported packets and explicitly catalogued packet paths are eligible; arbitrary filesystem documents cannot enter selection.

Constraint/input failures after optimizer evidence is frozen finish the saved run as failed with the original error; frozen inputs are preserved. The Showdown leader-history error names **Require at least one season-average non-QB team leader**, so users can deliberately refresh prior-week stats/snaps or change that preference. Status polls are scoped and monotonic by server time; a slower older response cannot replace newer preparation readiness.

Prepare Slate now checks regular-season stats and snap team coverage for every prior week, automatically captures and ingests only missing weeks/datasets, and validates coverage before rebuilding. Immutable source snapshots and ingest IDs are retained in `source_refresh.historical_participation`. An unpublished/incomplete provider slice blocks with retry guidance; user leader/rank rules remain unchanged.

For Showdown merged research, an underfilled initial candidate pool with too few distinct Captains for the requested Captain exposure now triggers a bounded unseen-Captain search against the same frozen legal model and authored scenario worlds. The report retains initial candidate count, added candidates and solver status in `captain_exposure_coverage`; eligibility, locks, scenario/focus requirements and exposure limits remain binding. This is candidate generation expansion, not a constraint relaxation or a controlled selector comparison.

Showdown **Optimization intent** now chooses Expected Game or Contrarian from separate automatically prepared receipts. One Prepare freezes both against the same source/model build; no manual packet selection or rebuild is needed between intent runs. [Intent workflow and comparison rules](analyst_optimization_intents.md) describe authored classification, unavailable-intent blocking and saved side-by-side results.
