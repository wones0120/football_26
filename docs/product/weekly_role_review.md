# Last week → this week role review

The optional `weekly_role_review_v1` packet block links previous-week native game, statistic row and player identities to current scoped canonical players. `scan_weekly_review(packet, rows, fmt=..., decision_at=...)` automatically screens every confirmed Classic/Showdown game in the prepared pool and reports missing history, event-sequence coverage and independent semantic findings.

Fumbles and fumbles lost remain separate. The screen covers box-score receiving opportunity and captured events: ball security, drops/assignment errors, post-event usage, crucial-down usage, availability, quarterback and protection changes. Aggregate statistics alone cannot establish benching, a promotion or quarterback trust. Events require native IDs or a reviewed native-roster crosswalk; raw names cannot join players. Missing game completion chronology remains explicit.

Findings record what happened, what coach/QB actually did or said, contradictory interpretations, role mechanism, conditions, model accounting, next checks and roster implications. At least two competing branches remain visible. Psychology such as “something to prove” is an unmeasured interpretation and cannot add fantasy points, change workload or force selection. A good day, touchdown or ball-security failure cannot automatically promote or demote a player.

`discovery_findings(packet)` bridges only a distinct sourced **measured_role** change into existing `analyst_discovery` bounded construction reservation controls. It requires verified completed-game evidence, matching factual assertions, partial/unmodeled accounting and a canonical required-player construction. Numeric transfers are prohibited by this block. Existing evidence thresholds, reserved-slot limits, user exclusions, locks, exposures and legal solver constraints stay authoritative. Qualitative conditional warnings produce watchlists, not numerical bonuses or penalties. Sources learned later create a new research edition; they never backfill earlier frozen cutoff knowledge.
