# Football awareness

Build lineups that can succeed in actual football games. Treat mathematics as the means to express and test football mechanisms, not as sufficient justification for a recommendation.

## Review the mechanism

Use current, canonically identified, time-appropriate evidence for roles, availability, quarterback and offensive-line context, opponent strength, pace and game environment. Separate observed facts, model estimates, user preferences and Analyst hypotheses. Missing evidence is not a healthy status or a zero workload.

Describe the full roster's path to success: which games produce opportunity, who receives it, whether opponents can respond, and which salary savings improve the remaining lineup. Compare another legal full roster under matched inputs and settings when recommending a construction change. Evaluate shared failure dependencies across all selected portfolios, including Expected and Contrarian together.

Check physical consistency of possessions, pass attempts, targets, carries, yards and scoring events. Receiving production must fit passing production; a passing touchdown is one physical event that can score fantasy points for both QB and receiver. Captain multipliers change fantasy scoring, not physical opportunities. Shares and budgets must vary with the scenario rather than freezing arbitrary ceilings.

## Evaluate likelihood honestly

Individual P90 values are marginal quantiles, not maxima; their sum is not lineup P90. Sum of means remains a valid expected score regardless of dependence, but does not establish tournament success probability. Preserve an explicitly requested highest-mean mode and label its limitations instead of secretly modifying its objective.

Consider both shared favorable environments and competition for opportunity. Do not impose generic same-team penalties, automatic stacks, quarterback reputation tiers or hard game-total cutoffs. Different-game alternatives also need an evidenced path to success.

Use joint calibrated outcomes when supported. Otherwise present conditional scenario sensitivity with unknown likelihood, not invented probabilities. Evaluate historical calibration, adequate sample sizes, dependence among entries and chronological provenance. Separate observed winners from pregame predictive evidence and do not tune a universal rule to one game.

## Reject and repair unsupported methods

Reject promotion when the mechanism contradicts evidence or football accounting, even if aggregate metrics improve. Examples include a shared-opportunity penalty clipping a lone player's legitimate touchdown/efficiency upside, or a model silently replacing missing home/history with zeros. A statistically inconclusive model is not proven harmful or superior; report the actual uncertainty and deployment standard.

Test meaningful invariants and plausible contrasting scenarios; compare chronological predictive results and full-lineup effects. Code tests alone do not validate football predictions. Do not bypass existing activation requirements or change live defaults merely to make results look differentiated.

Deliver a concise recommendation explaining the success path, supporting and contrary evidence, key shared risks, alternative, likelihood evidence/limitations, and whether the behavior is active or research-only. Make these reasons available in the relevant user workflow, not solely an unused research file.
