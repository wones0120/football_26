# Frozen retrospective protocol

Declared before outcome analysis: primary close-to-P90 threshold actual >= 0.90 × stored pregame P90; exact exceedance actual >= P90. Secondary sensitivity >=0.80 × P90. Require positive P90 >=5 DK points; report excluded smaller/zero/negative P90 separately. No positive-part rescaling. Include participants with zero actual points when participation evidence exists; nonparticipants retained as zero only if confirmed game outcome/participation semantics support it, otherwise unknown excluded with counts. No injury exclusions inferred after the game. Report pregame availability when stored, unknown explicitly.

Use canonical player IDs, canonical season/week/game/team, DK scoring definition/version, unmultiplied player forecasts (no Showdown CPT scaling). Contemporaneous storage requires immutable forecast/snapshot captured before applicable kickoff, not merely a requested historical cutoff. Prefer latest complete pregame source-run per player/game, deterministic ID tie-break; repeated optimizer selection does not multiply observations. Separate historical reconstructed/postgame/unknown chronology. Limit to already persisted data, no refit/backfill or live model changes.

First measure player marginal calibration, then pair joint rates for same-team WR-WR, WR-TE, RB-RB, RB-WR, QB-WR, QB-TE; opposing same-game and different-game position-matched controls. Compare observed joint rate to product of empirical side-specific marginals, not assumed 1%. Controls must share week/season, position pair and only available pregame projection/salary/opportunity/availability strata. Report unmatched and sparse groups. Do not tune implied-total/shootout cutoffs after outcomes. Report raw pair counts plus unique games/weeks; uncertainty clustered by week (and game sensitivity where supported), never pairwise IID confidence intervals. Describe pair dependence and selection/coverage bias.

Full-lineup implications are descriptive: contribution, salary tradeoff, and absolute joint success against matched different-game alternatives. No requirement that all nine players hit P90, no universal same-team penalty, no optimizer mutation. Previously inspected outcomes remain retrospective. If chronology or scoring evidence is insufficient, supported-subset results and prospective capture requirements are the result; no invented historical P90.
