# Observed offensive touchdown shares

G12-M04 player rushing-TD share and G12-M05 player receiving-TD share use full-source team rushing and passing/receiving TD denominators over exactly the same native games. All rushers and receivers count, including QB, non-QB, native unresolved and missing-identity residuals. The source-native td_player_id is authoritative, including lateral scores; a missing scorer with possible lateral is unattributed rather than donated to the original rusher/receiver. A known non-lateral can retain its original source actor. Newly observed native scorers remain present with identity review requests if the authenticated parent has no canonical attachment. A passing and receiving TD are one physical offensive score; team passing TD is never added again as a receiving score.

G12-M02 and G12-M03 are explicitly declared **attempt-denominator variants**: team rushing TD/all official rush attempts and passing TD/all official pass attempts. They are not TD-type shares of total offensive TDs. Official attempts include kneels/spikes; no-play and two-point exclusions follow the authenticated event taxonomy. These two IDs retain partial/variant classification until their denominator is accepted as the catalog definition.

Counters separate known counts, unknown event eligibility/outcomes and unknown native actors. Unknown TD attribution prevents exact player totals/shares, while preserving known full-team scores. A missing receiver on a known non-TD does not create a possible TD. Zero team TD means an undefined share, not a false zero probability. No role/replacement forecast or inferred end-zone intent is produced.

Actor windows pool the last3/8 observed canonical-player appearances on the same team, preserving every contributing native key (unresolved players remain separate native-key groups); the denominator contains precisely those same games, not unrelated recent team games. Absent actor games are not silently zero-filled. These are observed log windows, not proof of complete games. Source original availability/full-game completion remains unknown. Canonical identity comes from the authenticated native PBP parent; no raw-name joins or cross-team transfer is used.

The explicit retrospective reader authenticates original frozen bytes at their original evidence boundary, then retains actual postlock read/derivation/publication timestamps. The old boundary is not a new pregame cutoff. All current/pregame serving uses reject. Models, Analyst, optimizer and app consumers remain paused/pending under feature-only priority.
