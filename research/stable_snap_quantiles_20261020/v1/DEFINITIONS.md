# X02-M06/M07: empirical opportunity tails under prior snap stability

The source condition is explicitly predeclared before this response analysis: all immediately preceding three scheduled same-team/same-season games must have sourced positive offensive snaps, the same source-reported position, offensive-snap fraction at least 0.6 in each game and a fraction range at most 0.1. A floating comparison tolerance of 1e-12 applies only at the range boundary. This is stable observed snap participation, not proof of health, touch-role stability, future participation or a full-game role.

X02-M06 uses source-prior WR/TE/RB/FB eligibility and observed official target counts in the next scheduled game. X02-M07 uses source-prior QB/RB/FB and official carry counts (including kneels/scrambles). Response counts never qualify the same game's prior condition. First three scheduled games are not applicable; bye weeks cause no invented game, missing scheduled participation is not skipped or filled. A known failed conjunct proves exclusion despite other unknown inputs; otherwise missing prior count/fraction/role creates unknown eligibility. Native schedule and game IDs bind all source logs.

Per source-native PFR actor/team/season, retain every evaluated target slot. A unique canonical UUID links snap observations to reviewed source-native weekly counts; unresolved/conflicted identities retain native rows and existing issue evidence, with unknown response rather than a name join. Missing weekly response remains unknown, never DNP or zero. For qualifying response counts, q90 uses linear order statistics h=(n-1)*0.9. Cohort sizes and known-sample q90 are separate; any unknown condition or missing qualified response masks the full cohort quantile. A nonempty observed sample may be small and is not a future tail guarantee.

The existing retained snap revision already preserves offense_pct and position, so no new source projection is needed. Original equivalent revisions and native alias/collision receipts are retained. Numeric fractions must lie in [0,1], not silently rescaled. The provider's primary parser explicitly removes the percent sign and divides by 100. These are revised retrospective input observations; source publication and upstream preprocessing history remain unknown. Thresholds are declared for this analysis after historical outcomes were previously inspected, not a fresh blinded test. No model fitting, calibrated forward probability or optimizer/forecast serving is added.
