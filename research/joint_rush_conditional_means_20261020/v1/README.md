# Joint rush conditional means — prefit research only

This package implements one new offline model contract. It has not fitted a model, predicted a future roster, changed live forecasts, or modified lineups. The existing independent tree/neural consumers and their saved evidence remain unchanged.

The fixed prior-three scheduled-game all-role native candidate universe is retained, including prior-zero actors. Approved current source rows or exact event-credit zero proofs provide labels; missing labels are never imputed zero. The exact compatible canonical population has 330/343/311 groups in 2023/2024/2025. The conflicting HOU 2024 Week 15 carry counts remain excluded. This selected provider-observed population is not a complete NFL roster census or an unconditional future-participation expectation.

Each candidate and the disjoint numeric outside-candidate rest receives a softplus mean from ONE shared 8-unit tanh network. The budget mean is the sum of these means. No separately trained budget-times-share product, arbitrary rescaling, hard position zero, or donation of unknown mass is used. The rest may contain known unresolved source actors; unreported NFL mass remains unknown outside this assessed universe. Kneels, scrambles, and non-RB carries remain part of the official rushing channel.

The fixed recipe uses raw-count squared error: equal group weight, within-group mean cell error divided by a positive TRAIN-only cell-label variance scale, plus budget error divided by its positive TRAIN-only variance scale. Both standard deviations are floored at 1. A fixed .001 L2 penalty applies to weights, excluding biases. Optimization is one seeded L-BFGS-B run, at most 500 iterations, with frozen tolerances and no retry/grid. Nonconvergence blocks inference and preserves the failed status/parameters. This model is a conditional arithmetic-mean system; it is not a calibrated count distribution.

Inputs are the already reviewed 38 own/opponent prior-three weekly workload/efficiency fields, 29 candidate prior-three fields, a candidate/rest indicator, and prior candidate count. Every cell sees the same group context. Candidate features use native IDs and only the same three scheduled predecessors; current participation, role, counts, labels, group gate, and zero-proof flags are not predictors. Knownness fields describe PRIOR observations only. REST actor features are unknown rather than invented counts. Train-only imputation/standardization adds missing indicators; all-missing TRAIN columns stay unknown later. The 69 ordered inputs become 138 transformed fields.

`MANIFEST.json` binds exact compressed numeric tensors, every ordered group/cell key and label, training preprocessing, lineage, recipe, and saved-prediction intersections. The arrays have 44,499 cells and 984 budgets. `raw_inputs`, `X`, `labels`, `budgets`, `offsets`, and `group_years` are separate NPZ arrays. Metadata carries current label proofs solely for audit. No model is stored in these artifacts.

Saved pressure/log and direct-count tree/neural predictions are matched only on the same canonical candidate and unchanged observed rushing label. Each covers 3,081/3,242/3,055 candidates by year, with no fully covered candidate group. Missing predictions remain unknown and cannot be substituted for outside rest. Later evaluation must distinguish this partial old cohort from the new candidate/rest model; changed features, cohort, and loss confound attribution. Prior-history and TRAIN-only constant controls can be calculated without refitting old models.

Source captures, provider metrics, and aliases are revised retrospective evidence, not original-pregame availability proof. 2025 has repeatedly been inspected; it is not a fresh holdout. 2026 has no compatible event-cache support and is excluded. Targets and per-passer allocation are excluded from this root-assigned rush-only implementation. High must review the exact implementation/tensors; root must dispatch any fit separately. No activation is authorized.
