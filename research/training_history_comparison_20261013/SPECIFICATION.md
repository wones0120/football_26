# Frozen training-history comparison

Four arms for each fixed model: 2026 W1–4; 2025 plus 2026 W1–4; 2023–2026 W4 equal weight; the same full window with exponential recency weights. All training uses all games, REG rows, observed positive offensive snaps and observed labels. Primary assessment is 2026 W5 Sunday Main; all-game W5 is explicitly secondary. Missing labels stay missing. Source PPR is not DK scoring. Cohort is conditional on observed participation, not a pre-lock eligible universe or a DNP forecast.

Features are unchanged full-precision F3 role/PBP from the existing immutable matrix, with position encoding. They exclude target-game participation, outcomes, identity names, diagnostic flags and context feature additions. Player/team/opponent rolling history can cross seasons: the 2026-only arm restricts TRAINING EXAMPLES, not information used to construct their lag features. W1–4 and W5 lags can use 2025 and older records (especially mean8 and sparse player histories). This is not a strict no-older-data pipeline. No strict-2026 feature-history sensitivity is claimed; it would change the information set and is a separately designed follow-up.

Externally prescribed, identical across arms and targets: shared residual MLP (64,32), ReLU/Adam, learning rate .001, alpha .2, batch size 256, seed 261013, 100 epochs; HGB squared error, depth 5, 15 leaves, min leaf 60, l2 10, learning rate .05, 200 iterations, early stopping disabled. Configurations are informed by established capacity/tree research and older labels; 2026-only is not presented as a pipeline never influenced by old outcomes. Fixed budgets avoid sacrificing W4 or selecting epochs/decay on W5. No tuning or early stopping in this study. Fixed epochs imply more minibatch updates with larger windows; this optimization difference is reported and prevents attributing every NN difference solely to information quality.

Weights: age in calendar days since 2026 W4 latest kickoff, half-life 365 days, w=2**(-age/365); normalize mean weight to one within each target's training arm. Weight sum equals row count to keep sample-weight and regularization scales comparable. Actual MLP partial_fit sample_weight and HGB fit sample_weight are required. Same weighted preprocessing policy in every arm: numeric weighted median imputation, missing indicators, weighted train-only StandardScaler; train-only categorical encoder. Residual position priors and residual target scaler also use train-only sample weights. No W5 feature or label enters fitted preprocessing, priors, gradients or model selection.

Before fitting/scoring: persist this specification, script/dependency hashes, feature columns, versions, row/window manifests and exact weights. Save each checkpoint and its hash; reload and verify all predictions. Metrics: MAE/RMSE/prediction-minus-actual bias, position, prespecified realized-high-workload slices (targets>=8, carries>=15, PPR>=20), Spearman rank and PPR position NDCG/top-five overlap. Those outcome-defined subsets are diagnostic, not selectors. Every per-player error change versus 2026-only is saved; representative PPR cases selected mechanically as largest improvement, largest deterioration, and median absolute change, rather than narrative choice.

W5 has already been inspected repeatedly and all data are retrospectively reconstructed/revised. This is diagnostic retrospective evidence with no untouched holdout, original predecision availability receipt or future-performance claim. Existing source chronology audits are inherited with their limitations; period/completion/cutoff guards are checked again. A single W5 slate cannot settle the best training window. Repeat the unchanged arms at future weeks using genuinely predecision archived forecasts, canonical cohorts, source versions and scoring definitions, without changing arms after outcomes.

Only this study directory is written. No app, live projections, database, source artifacts or default configurations change. Bounded fit budget: 24 models and 100 NN epochs each, one native thread. Any failure is reported rather than adjusting the arm after inspecting assessment metrics.
