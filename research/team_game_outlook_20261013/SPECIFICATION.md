# Team/game outlook v1: frozen design

Revised-data retrospective using immutable expanded_v3 nflverse schedule/PBP captures. Historical imports are later revisions; source clocks do not establish contemporaneous backtest availability. Prior inspected outcomes are not independent holdout. Future W6 onward predictions have actual source receipt clocks and must precede decision/kickoff. No new data acquisition, production projection or default changes.

Transparent Ridge multi-output model. Features: home status, own and opponent last-five completed-game mean points for/against, scrimmage plays, offensive drives, EPA/play, success, target and carry fractions; rest days, own/opponent history counts. No team/player identity features, current game stats, market, weather realization or injuries reconstructed from today. Missing history train-only median; train-only scale. One alpha choice among 10 and 100 using 2024W1-8 validation points MAE after fitting 2023. Freeze chosen alpha before assessing 2025W3-17 / 2026W1-5. Final fit 2023-2025 and calibrate residuals on 2026W1-3; assess W4/W5 as already-inspected diagnostics. No alpha tuning on 2025/2026. Calibration for 2025 retrospective assessment uses 2024W9-18 residuals from 2023 fit, and final diagnostics use W1-3 calibration from 2023-2025 fit.

Targets: team points (all scoring), offensive scrimmage plays (PBP rush/pass attempt), offensive possessions (distinct fixed drives on offensive plays, includes kneel-ending drives), target fraction (receiver identified pass attempts/plays), carry fraction (rush attempts/plays). Targets include QB attempts and kneels, not only DFS skill volume. Volume outputs projected onto nonnegative feasible simplex: targets+carries<=plays; residual opportunity includes sacks/untargeted throws. Positive raw drive estimates clip to plays. No invented zero when PBP absent.

Joint game residual bootstrap: pair home and away residual vectors from same calibration game, seed261013, 4096 draws, rounded nonnegative team scores. Estimated win/lose/tie probabilities sum to1; ties are separate, win-half-tie comparison reported explicitly. No claim calibrated probabilities until measured calibration supports it. Shared residuals capture limited game covariance; not a drive simulator or fantasy lineup distribution.

Scripts: competitive final margin abs<=7; leading>7; trailing<-7. Opponent capability events: competitive_scoring=both scores>=20 and abs margin<=14; trailing_productive_passing=team trailing>7, points>=14, targets>=30; drive_stall_runout=team points<14 and points/drive<1.4. Events are explicitly descriptive proxies, can overlap, do not partition outcomes or prove QB/injury capability. Mutually exclusive capability branching for Analyst: competitive_scoring first; else trailing productive; else stall; else other. Script conditional budgets from same feasible draws; require >=30 draws or mark unavailable.

Metrics: points/total/margin/plays/drives MAE/RMSE, home-win-half-tie Brier/reliability bins, empirical 80% score interval coverage, explicit tie and script probabilities. Compare simple own/opponent scoring history baseline common rows. Sunday Main subgroup, all normal-week games. Model calibrations cannot assert reliable conditional/joint fantasy tails. No fantasy win/profit claims.

Consumer: explicit optional versioned saved evidence contract, immutable snapshot hash/model/features/training/calibration/metrics/seed/source clocks. Opt-in MERGED_SCENARIOS only; baseline projection truth preserved. Scenario volume factors are conditional budget/central budget, not a second market adjustment; conserve individual measured allocations and targets+carries<=team plays. Missing roster workload leaves unallocated budget rather than phantom opportunity. Analyst alternatives remain separate sensitivity hypotheses, uncalibrated portfolio weights. Legacy packets unchanged.
