# X06-M04: failed-third decision-opportunity contexts

Each source-proven failed-third opportunity uses its own pre-anchor nominal field position (yardline_100 <=35 versus >35), recognized half clock (<=120 regulation seconds versus earlier; overtime separately retained), and corroborated preplay ahead/tied/behind score. This is the reviewed X05-M08 anchor and competing-outcome universe, not a census of all field-goal decisions.

The full denominator includes all failed-third opportunities in the matched range × time × score cell. FG, punt, physical fourth-down attempt, clock end, possession end, penalty new series and observed new/repeated down remain separate. Unknown membership/outcome masks compatible exact full frequencies; known-outcome and choice-only samples remain explicitly separate. Empty cells are undefined, never zero. Original fourth-down spike/kneel subtypes are inherited in the bound anchor evidence.

Within matched time/score contexts, compare inside35 minus outside35; within matched range/score contexts compare late120 minus earlier regulation. Pool counts before division. Source-derived pre-anchor site is distinct from a later decision LOS, source endfield or actual kick distance. No kicker ability, aggression, causal response or future decision probability is claimed.

Actual coverage: 1,208 known anchors plus 19 possible unknowns, 158 game logs/32 profiles; 550 exact nonempty game cells/215 profile cells; 206 game and 118 profile categorical contrast cells. These cells overlap and are not independent observations or additional feature IDs. Prior source capture/lock and later feature publication clocks are retained. Revised historical availability and full NFL-game completeness remain unknown; all consumers and models stay paused.
