# Reported game-starting QB continuity and observed target opportunity

X03-M03 measures source-proven WR/TE opportunity in observed source player-week logs when the reported QB starter is the same as versus different from the immediately preceding scheduled same-team, same-season game. X03-M04 uses source-proven RB/FB logs. Current means each measured game. It does not mean a confirmed upcoming QB. Historical schedule identities are GSIS source keys; no volume-based starter inference or per-pass attribution.

For each actor/team/season and each group, report observed logs, known target sum, exact targets/log and pooled actor targets / identical-game all-native raw weekly source targets. Difference is changed minus same. First scheduled game is not applicable. Missing either starter is unknown, and the prior game is never skipped. Missing role/team evidence masks exact actor comparisons. Raw source rows with unresolved or malformed actor identity retain their team target budget; no user roster/filter affects it. Missing/unknown native team membership masks all possible same-period team budgets. Zero count is valid; empty group and zero share denominator are undefined. Known samples remain separate.

The source universe is observed official weekly rows, not all NFL active games. Missing source rows are never DNP/zero. Existing authenticated positive-offensive-snap participants without weekly rows are retained as unknown response evidence, including records without a native GSIS/role. Their compatible actor responses and same-game team budgets are masked; no role is inferred. Historical participation is not universally available, so this cohort is explicitly source-observed and no full-active-game completeness is claimed.

Frozen weekly revision and raw projection are current immutable research freezes of a replaceable legacy table, not original HTTP payloads. Native schedule publication history is unknown; source observations, actual reads, derivation and publication remain separate. Whole-game reported starter is descriptive context, not health/full-role proof, causal response or future probability. No model/optimizer consumption or serving.
