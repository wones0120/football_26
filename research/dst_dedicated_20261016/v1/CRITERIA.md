# Dedicated DST research protocol — frozen before fitting, 2026-10-16

This is a new model-design experiment after the corrected-label shared-tree refit failed. All 2025/2026 outcomes were previously inspected. This is retrospective chronological validation, not a fresh blind holdout. Captures reconstruct corrected historical truth; original historical pregame capture clocks remain incomplete. No live activation occurs in this task regardless of result.

Use the same canonical 804 DST matrix rows and verified event-score source as the prior experiment. Compare the incumbent shared-position GradientBoosting policy refitted on each original training window against dedicated DST-only learners fitted only on corrected prior DST outcomes. Both arms evaluate identical corrected outcomes, including genuine zeros and negative scores. The incumbent retains its existing 27-feature zero-fill/80:20-history/nonnegative prediction policy. Candidate direct DST predictions may be negative; no synthetic zero labels or prior unknowns become known zeros.

Candidate input policy: audited existing salary and market columns; home derived from independently checked canonical game ID (legacy home is constant zero); corrected preweek-only defense and opponent-conceded histories from the frozen complete event-score source (means, dispersion, sacks, takeaways and allowed points). No future actuals or current game's components enter features. No player names, numeric identity columns, newly inferred starters, opponent-QB features, constant position/offense features, or existing zero-filled DST history fields. Missing markets/history remain NaN until train-only median imputation with missingness indicators; observed zero is preserved. Audit all 27 incumbent columns separately and disclose unproven source timing.

Predeclare three learners, with no grid or follow-up tuning: training-label median DummyRegressor; StandardScaler + Ridge(alpha=100); HistGradientBoostingRegressor(squared_error, max_iter=100, max_leaf_nodes=7, max_depth=2, min_samples_leaf=30, l2_regularization=10, learning_rate=.05, random_state=42, early_stopping=False). Ridge/tree use the same train-only median+indicator preprocessing. Choose the lowest MAE on the last three available training weeks after fitting on earlier training weeks (ties prefer median, then ridge). This inner chronological choice is repeated within each outer training window. Outer calibration/test outcomes cannot select candidates. Report all inner scores and sample sizes.

Outer blocks match prior study: fit through 2024W13, calibrate remaining 2024, evaluate 2025; fit through 2025W14, calibrate remaining 2025, evaluate 2026W1–3 and separately previously inspected W4–5. Histories may use completed outcomes strictly before each decision week (including earlier test weeks), as a rolling operational feature would, while model parameters and calibration remain frozen per outer window. Exclude the decision week in its entirety. Never fit on outer test labels. Calibration uses 10th/90th prior residual quantiles for 80% intervals, identically for both arms.

Statistical gate (all required): canonical/scoring/version/cutoff audits pass; aggregate candidate MAE improves by at least .05 point versus incumbent; RMSE <= incumbent*1.02; combined 2026 MAE <= incumbent+.10; absolute aggregate bias <=1 point; 80% coverage between .70 and .90 and no >.05 coverage decline; 95% paired week-cluster bootstrap upper MAE difference <=+.20; average per-week Spearman ranking correlation no more than .10 below incumbent.
