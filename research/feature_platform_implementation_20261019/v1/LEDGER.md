These stages are not interchangeable. A blocker records exact missing input/access/definition, searched evidence, responsible owner and next action; it does not delete the candidate or stop independent packages.

1. Resolved definition/grain/units/denominator/window/applicability and parent ancestry; historical fact, feature, forecast, label and Analyst judgment remain separate.

4. Narrow tests, source/event/physical-accounting invariants, independent High review and documented limitations; predictive validation separate from mechanics.
5. Actual intended public-path saved evidence/report/export when serving changes, preserving model truth/preferences and standard control. No service restart/operational run by a worker without parent coordination.

Zone partitions and separate goal-to-go remain conserved. Sparse conditions use training-only definitions/shrinkage; no future realized context or invented probabilities.
