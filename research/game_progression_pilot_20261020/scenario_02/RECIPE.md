## Censored continuation: explicit observation requirement, no fitted model

The current recipe observes a source snap/action mark and either an exact wait W to the next timed action or right-censored followup C ending at the source half/regulation boundary. For a generated deadline R, C can establish no timed successor before the boundary when R<=C. When R>C, neither W beyond C nor a successor state was observed. Retain UNKNOWN_CENSORED_SUCCESSOR, report C, R, R-C and the native cycle ID, retain the already credited action mark once, and do not decrement the clock or copy the source boundary as a generated successor. An unrecognized wait-observation kind is explicitly unknown.

A kneel at the end of a source game is an informative policy choice depending on clock, score and timeout options. The existing early/late 120-second stratum and score sign do not establish that the same kneel/action policy applies at a longer generated horizon. Even a well-behaved marginal survival curve would not supply the missing joint action/wait/successor law. Do not pool exact waits from unrelated actions after sampling a censored mark, assume censoring independence, pick certified-only successors, or extrapolate the longest observed wait.

Additional observations required for a defensible continuation model:

- Native play-bound terminal state and enforcement: possession, phase, field coordinate, down/distance, typed points and all intervening fumble/lateral/penalty/untimed-try events. A following snap's pre-state is not a play-end timestamp.
- Game-clock state after the action: observed play end, running/stopped status and why, restart/runoff events, remaining timeouts for each side and their native use, quarter/half/regulation boundary and whether an untimed action is required. Preserve clock corrections explicitly.
- Observed longer-followup cases with the same action/terminal-state and relevant generated clock/score/timeouts/policy context. Require empirical support beyond each censored horizon. If the exact-match risk set has no followup, continuation remains unidentified; additional data alone do not guarantee support.
- Joint next action, its pre-state and wait linked to those observations; unresolved outcomes remain risk-set mass. Resolve known source-state gaps before declaring the successor law supported.

Any later model proposal must freeze its conditioning assumptions, action-policy mechanism, censoring mechanism and treatment of unknown tail/successor mass before fitting. Validate within TRAIN using disjoint chronological game windows, treating whole games as dependent units; use uncensored trajectories artificially censored at known cutoffs to check conditional wait coverage and successor mechanics separately. Report natural-censoring selection differences, tail sample sizes, unknown mass and transfer limits. Artificial censoring tests alone cannot validate informative endgame policy or identify an unobserved tail. Preserve action marks jointly so continuation cannot double-count opportunities or points. Do not use 2026 outcomes to select this mechanism. No such model is implemented or validated by SCENARIO-02.
