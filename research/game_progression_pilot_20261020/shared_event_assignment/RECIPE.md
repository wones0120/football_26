# Shared-event player opportunity assignment

Authorized 2026-10-21. Eight synthetic fixture cases only; one fixture game per ledger, explicit actor assignments, no probability model/draw, empirical fit, source request, source refresh or live consumer. Keep previous conditional vacancy fractions separate; they do not supply player-event probabilities. Preserve all prior pilot results and backend product-service Python hashes. No database/service, projection/settings, optimizer, saved lineup, production/scoring or timing-policy change.

Use the existing official opportunity channel names and unchanged event taxonomy as a cross-check. One targeted pass has one physical event identity and two linked channels: passer attempt and receiver target. Each channel has at most one integer actor credit on that event. Never count the two credits as two physical plays. Explicit no-target passes and spikes have attempts without target credits; sacks count as physical plays without official attempts/carries. Scrambles and kneels count as carries. No-play, two-point tries and controls remain separate retained records outside these official count/physical-play denominators. Physical counts refer to the official offensive-play counting scope, not all football actions.

Fixture-authored capabilities and conditional participation are assumptions, not game-active/role observations. No positional teammate caps or salary filters. A missing/outside canonical UUID, missing role capability, unknown participation or unavailable actor retains a known eligible opportunity in a residual actor bucket; it is not transferred to another player. Preserve requested UUID and reason. A known targeted pass with missing receiver has a known target residual; a pass with unknown target eligibility has an unknown target denominator, not a zero/no-target assertion. Unknown event mechanism retains unknown physical count and eligibility in every channel. Per-player counts are assigned lower bounds with explicit team-channel eligibility-unknown flags, not complete totals when evidence is missing.

Freeze cases for mixed supported mechanisms, unresolved receiver, unknown target eligibility, unknown event mechanism, unknown role, unknown participation, unavailable actor and unmapped UUID. Canonical identity is UUID-only; duplicate event/actor IDs, multiple receivers per slot, multiple fixture games, conflicting actor slots and cross-team assignments fail. Preferences do not alter football credit. No yard, reception, touchdown or fantasy-point outcomes are assigned in this slice.

One eight-case audit has a 10-second internal cap, 30-second external timeout, exclusive run/output creation and no automatic retry. Verify unique shared event/channel credits, player/residual conservation for every known opportunity, and exact-versus-lower-bound/unknown semantics. Target new assignment tests, existing observed event tests and recent vacancy/reference/role/accounting tests. Evidence remains synthetic mechanics, not a simulation rerun or forecast improvement claim.
