# X02-M09/M10 — observed full-team allocation covariance

For each team, use at most8 strictly prior observed current-season native team games from the reviewed full-source opportunity component. Target allocation (M09) and official rushing allocation (M10) are separate channels. Rushing includes every measured native rusher, including QB opportunities, kneels and scrambles, without role/position/salary/user-preference filtering. Canonically unresolved native actors retain their counts/coordinate. No arbitrary teammate limits or negative-correlation assumption is imposed.

Freeze the coordinate universe as the union of native actors with known positive official channel counts in these logs, plus an explicit unattributed-native-residual coordinate. For a complete, positive team count, with no native attribution gap, actor share is that actor's observed official count / team count. An actor without an event in this complete source log has known zero channel opportunities in the observed log; this is not a DNP/activity assertion. The residual coordinate is then exactly0. All shares sum1.

Known native-attribution residual may belong to an already known actor, so a positive residual masks the ENTIRE exact allocation vector, not just the residual coordinate. Unknown eligibility or a zero channel denominator likewise prevents exact share vector construction. Actor known counts, team counts, residual lower bounds and uncertainty flags remain visible for every game. No residual is donated or treated as independent workload. All actor counts are reconciled against full-source team budgets by the reviewed X08 diagnostics.

For n>=2 known complete game vectors, report pairwise sample covariance of allocation shares using divisor n−1, units share². The complete-window covariance is published only when every selected observed team log has a complete vector; otherwise it remains null, with the known-complete-sample covariance separately labeled. n<2 remains undefined. Covariance row sums must be zero within numerical tolerance because source shares conserve1; this is a mechanical accounting constraint, not fitted dependence or predictive calibration.

This is empirical same-team/season source allocation variation only, not a future joint outcome distribution, event independence assumption or probability forecast. Observed game logs are not independently certified full-game/league completeness. No historical pregame source availability is invented: original source boundary and current retrospective read/derive/publication are separate. The authenticated existing component/raw/compressed-parent reader is reused with no parent inflation. No model, optimizer, scenario or saved lineup consumption.
