**The clearest actual salary-reinvestment alternative is Wan’Dale Robinson $4,500: his $2,200 savings fund Hubbard→Henry (+$1,900) and Otton→Higbee (+$100), leaving $200.** The full lineup rises from **131.57 to 133.18 unchanged base points (+1.60)** and from 168.67 to 169.10 on the **same** saved LAR-passing conditional objective (+0.43). Adams is the highest conditional-objective replacement, but his $100 discount is left unspent and the result commits four players/$27,900 to the Rams. Nabers plus Higbee is another role-supported alternative.

**There is one material construction distinction.** The original normal LAR idea required an Arizona receiver; the user's saved stack setting did not (`bringback=false`). If we keep that inferred Arizona response requirement, **none** of the tested replacements improves the conditional objective. The promising alternatives below use a separate, explicitly labeled sensitivity removing that one scenario requirement. They preserve the normal LAR-passing world, all user settings and all other scenario constraints. This changes the game story from “Rams plus Arizona response” to “Rams production without requiring an Arizona fantasy scorer.” No user constraint was relaxed.

**Why the savings can help, and what they cost in football terms:**

- **Wan’Dale→Henry/Higbee is my preferred redesign to review.** Wan’Dale has 7.67 recent observed targets and 75% Week 5 snaps; it is a receiving role at a lower salary rather than a low-snap punt. Henry averaged 24.33 recent carries and retains the frozen normal Baltimore run-control thesis. Higbee's 7.67 recent targets/67% Week 5 snaps buy a second Stafford receiver for only $100 over Otton; Rams Allen DNP/Parkinson LP make that role worth checking, without assuming either is out. The trade loses Hubbard's observed 84% lead-back role and uses a fourth Ram, increasing own-team concentration from 42.6% to 49.4% of the cap even as combined ARI/LAR exposure falls from 56% to 49.4%. Henry's 55% Week 5 snap share and limited receiving, Lamar's unresolved status, plus Tennessee's modest 18.75 implied points are real counterweights. [Frozen workload/source report](../../sunday_refresh_20261015/v 2/RESEARCH.md), [Rams estimated Wednesday report](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/injury-report-week-6-vs-rams).
- **Nabers/Higbee is the smaller redesign.** Nabers has 11 recent targets/game and 90% Week 5 snaps; the [Giants' current preview](https://www.giants.com/news/week-6-new-orleans-saints-john-harbaugh-jameis-winston-malik-nabers-isaiah-likely-dennard-wilson-tyler-shough) confirms productive Nabers/Likely involvement. The $200 saving buys Higbee without giving up Hubbard. It also increases Rams own-team concentration to 49.4%. Winston's efficiency and both Giants tackles' Wednesday DNP threaten realization; Likely competes for valuable targets. Nothing here declares the tackles out or Nabers finally cleared for Sunday.
- **Adams is the cleanest mathematical swap, but barely a salary play.** Recent 9.67 targets and 73% Week 5 snaps support an established role. His frozen base mean is 14.59 versus Wilson 14.40, and the same Rams-passing world increases his conditional score. Four Rams account for 55.8% of salary; Puka and Adams compete for targets, Kyren for TD allocation, and a poor Rams offensive day hurts every one. It removes Arizona's ability to respond as a roster requirement, not the need for enough Rams volume. The $100 cannot fund a better DST/TE without a further trade; the matched objective prefers leaving it unused.
- **The cheaper punts do not establish an improvement.** Worthy's reinvestment barely changes base mean and loses conditional objective; Rice DNP alone is not an earned target transfer. Treadwell's roughly 5 targets/80% snaps are more credible than an unused backup, but his 5.55 base mean leaves touchdown/efficiency dependence; the apparent+0.25 base gain still loses the chosen objective. Bernard's injury-supported role (92% Week 5 snaps) is legitimate, but his 4 recent targets and unchanged 6.01 base mean do not recover the full-roster cost. Lemon's 5.67 targets/81% snaps remain sensitive to Philadelphia receiver returns. No injured teammate was newly declared out, no receiving bonus was invented, and no user Q exclusion was treated as an actual vacancy. [PIT personnel](https://www.steelers.com/news/week-6-injury-report-buccaneers-x 2319), [PHI Wednesday report](https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/news/panthers-vs-eagles-injury-report-2026-nfl-week-6-saquon-barkley-devonta-smith).

**Keeping the original Arizona-response requirement changes the answer.** The optimizer's strongest Wilson-excluded roster is Stafford/Hubbard/Kyren/Puka/**Virgil $3,000**/Henry/Higbee/Odunze/Panthers, salary $49,600, base 131.35(−0.23), conditional objective 165.87(−2.81). It funds Henry and Higbee partly by removing both Wilson and Tate, with Virgil at only 18% Week 5 snaps despite five recent targets. That is an explicitly fragile role punt, not proof that cheap Arizona support improves this lineup. Forcing the more established **Bourne $4,000** produces $50,000/base 129.66(−1.91)/objective 164.18(−4.49): Stafford/Kyren/Henry/Puka/Tate/Odunze/Higbee/Bourne/Ravens. McBride alternatives have to fund a $7,200 TE under the single-TE setting. The strict original-story answer is **keep Wilson**.

My recommendation is to retain the original saved lineup as the original record and carry **Wan’Dale/Henry/Higbee** and **Nabers/Higbee** as reviewable replacements if an Arizona bringback is optional. Adams is the objective leader but adds concentration; it is not evidence that larger salary savings improve the roster. These small gains have not been forward validated and do not establish payout superiority or final entry readiness.

**Reproduction and limits.** This is a bounded **generation experiment**, not an identical-pool portfolio-selector test. It cloned the exact saved 202-player PuLP legal model, player moments, salary/projection/ownership snapshot and normal LAR world; no live refresh or model change occurred. Stafford/Puka/Kyren were fixed in every solve. The original normal run explored six candidates and saved five; this study made 29 CBC solves (seed 7,12-second per-solve limit), all completed Optimal, producing 25 distinct canonical research rosters. Twelve forced replacement WRs were tested in each arm, plus unrestricted Wilson-exclusion and optimized Wilson-retained controls; exact baseline was separately replayed. The retained-Wilson optimum reproduced the original roster in both arms. Thornton was absent from the exact eligible pool and was not admitted.

The original single-TE, favored RB/DST, no offense against DST, exclude-Q/UNKNOWN gates, QB/RB $4,500 minima, four/team, five/game and DEN exclusion were retained through the frozen legal model. The original request-only **no rank restriction** remains precisely as used in the baseline; stored rank defaults were not changed. User exposure was 100%; these are independent candidate alternatives, not a new five-lineup portfolio. Portfolio-wide receiving-pair/triple diversity cannot be claimed for this single-lineup study. The main arm retains all LAR scenario constraints; the optional arm explicitly removes only its inferred ARI-response constraint and reports that relaxation separately in machine evidence. No other eligibility, source-health or construction relaxation occurred.
