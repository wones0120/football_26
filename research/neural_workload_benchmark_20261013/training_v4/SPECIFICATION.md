## Selection, metrics and uncertainty
Selection score = equal-position mean MAE divided by max(1,training-position mean absolute target). This stops dominant observed-zero positions from being the only objective; no promise it improves pooled MAE. Report raw pooled/position MAE, RMSE, signed bias, observedzero/positive, high workloads targets>=6/carries>=10/PPR>=15, sparse history<=1, prior-team change and workload shifts>=4, Sunday player/breakout rank NDCG/topK and QB–WR/TE pair-sum diagnostics as in V3. Compare each feature family, fold and diagnostic week rather than picking a favorable week/target.

Uncertainty: after selection, dedicated later calibration week supplies absolute residual finite-sample90% interval radius per position (min30 rows; otherwise pooled). Empirical subsequent coverage, width and upper/lower miss rates are evaluated for each model on identical rows. Workload intervals floor at0, PPR does not. Chronological drift, small calibration groups and retrospective reuse invalidate any guarantee of conditional/forward/joint calibration; report observed coverage only. These residual intervals do not train quantile/ceiling heads or model correlated lineups. Mean/upper-bound ranking within a position with constant radius adds no distinct tail signal.
