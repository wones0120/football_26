# V2: revised-data retrospective comparison

Frozen before prediction evaluation, 2026-10-13. User explicitly approved comparing an older historical week and starting broader data inputs. This version supersedes V1's ingestion-clock eligibility restriction only for this separate retrospective study. V1 artifacts remain unchanged. Late ingestion of a completed historical game's statistics is permitted; contemporary version/provenance is unavailable and revisions are possible. This is not a recreation of past app behavior or a fresh blind test.

## Fixed question, population and week
Main tasks: next-game observed targets and carries. Auxiliary: source-defined `fantasy_points_ppr`, not DraftKings scoring or production projection postprocessing. Fit separate regressors for each label. Population: uniquely canonical-mapped QB/RB/WR/TE with an observed REG weekly stat row and nonmissing label. No claim of documented positive offensive snaps. Absent rows/DNP remain absent; numeric zeros in observed records remain zero. Conditional recorded-player selection creates survivorship bias; report it.

Choose the most recent complete prior-season REG week by row/week coverage alone: 2025 Week18 (18 observed weeks). Train 2023 and 2024 plus 2025 W1–12; validation2025 W13–17; test2025 W18. Whole week blocks; no test/validation weeks in training. No tuning: validation reports only. Cross-season prior-three appearances allowed, with elapsed calendar-week surrogate for gap diagnostics; current season history count separate. Current-season workload rows are not used. Target labels never enter same-game inputs; team and opponent aggregate features only use strictly earlier season/week blocks.

## Paired feature treatments
Common baseline: prior-three/last targets, carries, PPR, last-minus-mean target/carry trends, prior-three player workload shares, prior team skill targets/carries, history count, weeks since prior appearance, position. These are developmental baseline features, not the production feature contract.

Enriched adds prior-three receptions, receiving/rushing yards and TDs, passing attempts/yards/TDs/interceptions, receiving air yards, catch rate, yards/target, yards/carry, passer yards/attempt, prior team pass attempts/pass fraction proxy, opponent prior-three positional PPR allowed and targets/carries allowed. Ratios use prior aggregate numerators/denominators; undefined denominators remain missing. This is a deliberately small football set rather than all numeric columns. Train-only median imputation with availability flags and standardization; position one-hot. No player/team/opponent identity one-hot.

Source current game/team/opponent linkage is retrospective, reconstructed metadata. Historical team volume follows last documented prior player team, preventing current outcome team membership from selecting a newly transferred player's contextual totals. Target opponent is reconstructed matchup metadata, not target-game defensive production. Team-change cases are diagnostics only. Do not use historic news/injury/depth/rosters/markets without actual historical evidence. Snaps/routes/red-zone and situational play-by-play families are unavailable here and stay missing/excluded rather than fabricated.

## Fixed models and limits
History = prior-three mean per target, fallback train-position mean. Tree = HistGradientBoostingRegressor(max_iter100,max_leaf_nodes15,learning_rate0.05,l2_regularization1,min_samples_leaf30,early_stoppingFalse,random_state261013). Neural = MLPRegressor((16,8),relu,Adam,alpha0.01,learning_rate_init0.001,batch_size128,max_iter150,early_stoppingFalse,shuffleFalse,random_state261013), train-standardized target. No target/nonnegative clamp for PPR (negative real scores valid); zero-clamp targets/carries for all models. One CPU thread, 120 seconds/model, no sweeps. Both tree and NN get byte-identical baseline matrices, then byte-identical enriched matrices and identical measured target samples/windows. No feature-set change selected from test scores.

## Metrics before scoring
Targets/carries/PPR MAE, RMSE, signed bias, residual quantiles, by-position; observed zero vs positive workload; high targets>=6, carries>=10, PPR>=15 vs lower; sparse prior history<=1 vs2+; current-team change and workload shift absolute label-minus-last>=4. Groups<30 are descriptive only. Partial-population team aggregation plausibility and +20% prior workload sensitivity are diagnostic. Neural follow-up encouraged only if test MAE improves >=5% vs both history and paired tree for BOTH main workload targets; a one-week result never proves superiority or future profitability. Show every test player's actuals and all predictions in CSV, plus a deterministic illustrative shortlist selected by prior target/carry workload, not outcomes.

Hash sources, feature matrices, labels, code/config; record sample/time/coverage and training clocks by week. Preserve native-to-canonical ambiguity and LA/LAR normalization checks. Audit production explicit model features vs contextual inputs/postprocessing; presence of a hook is not measured historical coverage. No live writes, new dependencies, production/default changes or winner promotion.
