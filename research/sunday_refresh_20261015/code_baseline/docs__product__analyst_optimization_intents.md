Each story/Captain targets `max(2, analyst_candidates_per_thesis)` distinct role rosters, counting existing candidates. Canonical player ID plus CPT/FLEX defines uniqueness. The search retains the existing opportunity, position and named-exception, salary, leader, correlation and story gates; it does not add low-role players solely to fill a diversity quota. Every result still competes under the unchanged conditional scenario portfolio objective.

Search passes give every eligible Captain/story a first look before additional supporting alternatives. The hard budget is 128 added solves with a five-second limit per solve. Only proven-optimal solves contribute; infeasible or unproven branches and exhausted budgets remain explicit. `captain_candidate_search` records the policy, per-Captain target, eligible IDs, actual attempts, status counts and story/Captain coverage; `generation` retains each attempted fixed Captain and solver status. The initial global budget remains separately recorded. Full exhaustive legal-roster enumeration is not claimed.

Captain exposure is still the configured maximum, not a requirement to repeat Captains. A 50% limit over four lineups permits at most two Captain appearances per player. Changing search and exposure together is a combined generation/selection treatment; compare on the same prepared build and retain the prior runs. Different pools do not constitute a fixed-pool selector experiment or establish calibrated EV. Classic, legacy mixed research and original CONSTRUCTION retain their existing search behavior.
