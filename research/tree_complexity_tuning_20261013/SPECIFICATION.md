# Frozen retrospective tree complexity study

Scope: research-only HistGradientBoosting, not the production pipeline. No live model, projection, default, database, future W6+ label, or upstream artifact writes. Existing revised historical data and previously inspected outcomes make every result retrospective.

Freeze before fits: F2_efficiency and F3_role_pbp; targets, carries, source fantasy_points_ppr. V4 A_2024, B_2025, C_2025 whole-week fit/stop/select/calibrate/assessment partitions unchanged. Assessment = every labeled retrospectively positive-snap Sunday Main participant; DNP participation is not forecast. Exact feature matrix is context full-precision parquet, using base features only. V4 neural F2 outputs used rounded source CSV; F3 context baseline neural outputs used this exact matrix. Retain both without refitting or choosing a neural winner from assessment.

180 maximum fits = three folds × three targets × two feature families × ten settings. Two old controls fit their unchanged fixed 150/200 iteration settings and are selected on past selection score. Eight tuned settings (listed in run.py) span max depth 3/5/8/unlimited, max leaves 8/15/31/63, leaf minimum15/30/60, L2 0/1/2/10, learning rates .03/.05/.08, cap300/400 iterations. This bounded joint design is not a full factorial and cannot isolate every interaction. Depth trio shares all settings except depth/leaves. Shared position-one-hot model only; no position-specific extra search.

Train-only median/missingness/scaling/position preprocessing, same V4 workload floor and position-normalized MAE selection. For tuned models evaluate each25-iteration stage on stopping weeks, retain earliest minimum, stop after four stale checkpoints once50iterations fitted; max400. Selection week chooses earliest minimum config. Neither calibration nor assessment enters stopping/selection; no refit on stopping/selection. Calibration uses same residual90% interval procedure. Hard120second per-fit and1200second total training cap; timeout means explicit failure, never silent smaller-budget winner.

Report old/tuned pooled and perweek MAE/RMSE/bias, position/highworkload/history/teamchange/workloadshift, PPR ranking/pairs, whole-week paired bootstrap (2000draws fixed seed261013), train-stop curves and actual depth/leaves/iterations/runtime. Highworkload/workloadshift are outcome-defined diagnostic strata, never features. Twelve dependent assessment weeks cannot establish untouched holdout significance, profitability, causal complexity effects, intrinsic architecture superiority, or production improvement. Larger tree tuning budget than neural two-config comparisons must be disclosed. Retain frozen neural comparator cells separately; any best neural cell shown descriptively is best-of-column after historical inspection.

Persist pre-fit freeze with config/input/code/dependency hashes, every validation choice, selected model/checkpoint, exact fullprecision predictions, metrics and replay checks. Immutable source lineage is inherited from context and V3 receipts; reconstructed chronology does not prove original availability. No unsupported historical injuries or same-game outcomes in predictors. No prospective or production promotion in this task.
