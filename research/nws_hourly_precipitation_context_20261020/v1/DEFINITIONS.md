# Explicit hourly forecast precipitation probability (G20-M07)

One declared source-cohort NWS hourly forecast supplies precipitation probability for the unique native game whose kickoff is in the source interval, with a verified native venue point covered by the source grid Polygon. This is an externally reported weather forecast, not permission to train or activate our models. The NWS [forecast terms](https://www.weather.gov/ppg/forecast_terms) define precipitation probability for a specified period and point, expressed as percent. It is not observed rain, precipitation amount/type, the fraction of an hour with rain, or the proportion of the venue area receiving rain.

Select exactly one offset-aware interval with start <= kickoff < end and exactly one hour duration. Native unit must be `wmoUnit:percent`, finite 0–100; fraction = percent/100 explicitly. Missing/invalid/ambiguous probability stays unknown; zero is a valid sourced forecast. Generated/update clocks must precede original capture, capture must precede both native kickoff and slate lock; generated forecast age at kickoff is predeclared at most 24 hours. A malformed/unlocatable interval could be competing evidence and masks the exact value. No period interpolation or selection by nearest time.

Only the actual raw GeoJSON Polygon is used, never a nearby office/city name. Boundary points are included, hole interiors excluded; malformed rings, absent coordinates, unsupported geometry and dateline-spanning rings are unknown. The unique native schedule game is bound by season/week, kickoff equal to original slate lock, deterministic native venue mapping and point-in-grid proof. The source-controlled venue registry, reviewed native overrides and frozen schedule are inherited from the separately reviewed venue package. Names never resolve venues or players.

The original immutable indexed NWS JSON/manifest and their prelock clocks are authenticated. The native game/venue attribution uses a later revised schedule and reviewed registry; original weather receipt availability is separate from all-input original-pregame proof. One weather observation creates two team contexts, not two independent observations. No calibration claim, outcome inspection validation, observed-rain substitution, wind/gust inference, player identity mutation, source capture, model/optimizer activation or serving.
