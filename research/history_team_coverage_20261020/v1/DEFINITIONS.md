# X08-M06 — observed prior same-team / other-team history coverage

Feature-only diagnostic at each historical native player/team/REG period in the reviewed frozen weekly source. Select at most8 strictly earlier observed native-player weekly logs, across seasons; identify each prior period/team explicitly. The current reference row's opportunity outcome is excluded. Same-period records with ambiguous chronology cannot be ordered by team spelling or clipped arbitrarily at the window boundary. All joins are native GSIS keys plus preserved canonical attachments, never names. No current roster, active role or future team membership is inferred from a latest team.

Report the fraction of observed prior source logs on the reference row's team, plus per official pass/rush/target exposure channel: same-team and observed-other-team known exposure sums, unknown exposure log counts, exact sums where supported, and same-team / total prior exposure fraction when positive and fully classified. Passing counts require the original source-proven QB exposure; non-QB zero passes are not invented. Unknown team membership or missing counts masks the exact fraction; zero exposure denominator is undefined. No prior logs means undefined, not perfect same-team coverage. Cross-season records are explicit descriptive ancestry, not a same-regime assertion.

“Other-team observed history” indicates source history available on another team. It is not proof any model transported that history, should transport it, or can preserve its role. Native/canonical gaps and prior durable queue receipts are inherited. Missing provider games are not DNP or zero; the population is observed source logs, not all scheduled games or a complete-career guarantee. Canonical-unresolved native histories remain visible with measured source exposure.

The source is the immutable current revision of the retained raw weekly table, not an original historical HTTP capture. All original source IDs, ingest/import clocks, aliases and quarantine proof stay linked through the verified input component/extract hashes. Original historical publication chronology remains unknown, actual read/derive/publication are current, and this diagnostic cannot serve models/forecasts/optimizer/scenarios. There are no new sources, fits, database writes or saved lineup/settings changes.

Compact storage: each prior native period is `[season, week, team]`, with field names declared once at the artifact top level. Unit definitions are stored once, and same/other team log counts are shared across channels. This preserves exact source membership while avoiding repeated schema labels across60k reference rows. The first oversized output attempt published nothing; no source cap was raised and no original source was replaced.

Unknown prior team membership masks global exact fractions and sums, while already-classified same-team/other-team sample log counts and exposure lower bounds remain visible. Unknown membership never wipes known sample evidence. The exact catalog transported-input coverage remains separate: this package reports available other-team source history, not evidence that a forecast actually consumed it; proposed feature status is partial pending that distinct consumer ancestry.
