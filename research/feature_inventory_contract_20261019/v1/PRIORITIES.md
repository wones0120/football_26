2. **Explicit forward counts now have a real serving path, separate from FP truth.** Monday's frozen Prepare `0492fea9-c01a-487f-86f7-d54f69e697c4` served standard, workload and matched offensive-FP control, all reopened with valid CSV responses. Daniels' pass/rush and McLaurin's targets are actual conditional producer forecasts; verified inactive gaps repaired abstention without zero fill. See `research/workload_count_optimizer_20261019/v1/REVIEW.md:3–5,44–56`. This closes the Batch A code-only uncertainty for this specific current receipt, not all actors, historical cohorts or Classic serving. Count errors are mixed across positions/channels; there is no demonstrated general superiority.
3. **Missing context fields and missing forecasts are different gaps.** B1's saved Monday sample has 60 canonical context actors, 16 practice observations, zero populated practice-trend/expected-workload-status/red-zone-share/goal-line-share fields (`batch_b1_evidence_receipt.json`, `saved_current.context_coverage`). A separate frozen count receipt exists. Neither a null context slot nor an ACT roster row proves zero workload, healthy status or missing counts everywhere. Typed final designation resolution and verified historical inactive evidence already exist and should be reused, not rebuilt.
4. **The automatic Analyst consumer works, but fresh football reasoning is not guaranteed.** The actual Monday standard run still uses a neutral reference scenario; `analyst_preparation.py:149–155` explicitly records `semantic_research_refreshed=False`. Rechecking scope/clocks is not researching affected roles or opponent response. The previously verified producer trace is in `research/football_pipeline_integration_review_20261019/v1/REVIEW.md:7–51`; current acceptance confirms the same limitation in `research/workload_count_optimizer_20261019/v1/REVIEW.md:50`. No new agent framework or paid API inside the solver is necessary to close this loop.
5. **Existing source bytes support useful next steps without buying charting.** B1 verified 13,842 PBP rows across 79 games (2026 W1–5), including field position, goal-to-go, passer/receiver/rusher IDs, TD and drive/kick components, plus 7,370 snap rows. B2 verified populated EPA/success/CPOE/YAC and sack/QB-hit components in a bounded game. These are revised historical captures, not original pregame publication proof, and full canonical joins/eligible denominators remain unverified. Routes, first-read/alignment/coverage, complete pressures/contact and actual matchup assignments remain unknown in the inspected sources; sacks or FP allowed are different measures, not replacements for those fields.
6. **Whole-roster machinery exists, but calibrated joint ceiling probability does not.** Active soft correlation terms have unvalidated policy weights; scenario generation/coverage exists; normalized tail-demand, copula and physical-event simulators are research variants with explicit limitations. None establishes a calibrated probability that several selected players reach useful ceilings. Marginal mean sums remain valid expected-score comparisons; marginal P90 sums are not joint P90. See `audits/lineup_integration.md`, J1–J11 and G24 summary.

Two definition corrections matter before reuse: the research simulator's `goal_line_multiplier` triggers at position >=90 from its own goal, so it means inside-ten, not catalog inside-five or goal-to-go (`research_game_simulation.py:199–203`). This is a specific research-mechanism mismatch, not evidence all production goal-line fields share that definition. Also, G17-M06 and G22-M04 are the same snap-trend ancestry; preserve both catalog entries but reuse one measured definition rather than double-counting independent evidence.

In the same wave, finish rank 2 for one affected-game mechanism through the existing producer/import/Prepare path and save the complete-roster explanation. Existing FP/count arms remain controls. **Wave 2 — add one coherent scoring-opportunity/stat vertical.** Scope rank 3 to the three disjoint zones, separate goal-to-go and a small yard/TD component path. Evaluate a single small opponent/context block from rank 4 only after event definitions and source coverage are sound. Keep sparse-bin shrinkage, train-only condition features and uncertainty explicit. Do not add every split or chase a known winning roster. This is the first new-model wave, subject to the user's later approval of definitions/scope.

**Wave 3 — complete the advanced-source/deployment workstream while preparing broader integration.** Finish route/alignment/pressure/contact/protection/matchup source investigations and exact definitions for every assigned long-tail ID; implement measured sources where verified and carry explicit access/definition decisions where blocked. Source investigation may start in parallel with Wave 1, without purchases. W4 then completes context interactions and rank 5's shared scoring/world contract, canonical candidate union and persisted final recommendation. W5 continuously evaluates feature blocks and regularization. These stages retain the entire catalog; a field not chosen by one fitted model remains available in the platform with its measured relevance/limitations. Calibrated joint probability remains a distinct claim requiring evidence.

## How to measure meaningful improvement

- **Correctness:** fewer demonstrably wrong identities/statuses/windows, no future leakage, complete physical accounting, explicit source/unknown residuals, and preserved user/model separation. A repaired fact is worthwhile even if a prior inflated projection falls.
- **Forecast utility:** evaluate opportunity counts, then yards/TD/FP separately on fixed chronological blocks. Report coverage/abstention, signed bias, count/yard error and relevant probability calibration; meaningful-role cohorts and returning/new-team/sparse roles matter more than a benchmark dominated by easy backup zeros. Compare simple declared baselines. Fit preprocessing, bins and shrinkage on training data only. No claim that retrospective reconstruction restores original pregame availability.
- **Selection utility:** compare complete legal rosters and all requested portfolio entries under identical frozen inputs/settings. Hold candidate pool fixed for selector tests; label candidate-generation changes separately. Report distinct CPT-aware rosters, requested/achieved size, solver/constraint outcomes, baseline FP differences, salary tradeoffs and shared failure sensitivities. A higher count utility or narrative plausibility alone is not a higher fantasy expectation or contest success probability.
- **End-to-end utility:** actual public Prepare → Optimize → reopen saved results → valid native CSV; persisted component/packet/source versions, selected policy, exclusions, assumptions, alternatives and contradictions must be visible. Research enumeration or a unit test alone is insufficient.

Use existing 2023–25 sources/saved predictions where their contracts and cutoffs are supportable; do not invent a blanket lack-of-data blocker. Current 2026 W1–5 and previously inspected contest outcomes are not fresh held-out evidence. Freeze later comparisons prospectively, keep already inspected cases as regressions, and maintain independent game/week counts rather than treating millions of repeated lineups as independent samples. Existing count validation and identical-source allocation replay are mixed/negative in places; preserve those contradictions and do not retune until they disappear.

## Later stages and explicit classification — no wishlist removal

- Route/alignment/first-read/man-zone/pressure/contact/win-rate data: access and exact local measurement are unknown after scoped searches, not proven universally unavailable. No vendor purchase recommendation follows from this inventory.
- Hundreds of situation splits and context interactions: retain in W4 and the extension registry with exposure/sample/shrinkage definitions. Build the library broadly, then test grouped model inclusion. Do not silently drop candidates because their utility is not yet proven, or equate a 320-item inventory with 320 statistically independent signals.
- Weather into optimizer weights: current display-only boundary is intentional (`test_weather_decision_boundary.py:7–45`). Forecast and realized weather have different clocks/units; any future serving change needs its own justified time-safe comparison.
- Kicker/DST bans, hard teammate rules, reputation tiers or forced injury opportunity transfers: not remedies for missing models. Kicker history/role fallback and the **selected** DST serving component must be assessed as they actually run. A nested candidate metadata flag `live_enabled=false` does not override the saved outer DST `status=selected`, `policy=ensemble` receipt for two current rows (`batch_b1_evidence_receipt.json`, `saved_current.dst_deployment`; `dst_deployment.py:24–43,78–103`).
- Artificial certainty: role-evidence completeness/confidence labels, heuristic weights, sampled Monte Carlo error and calibrated predictive uncertainty are different quantities. Keep unknown probabilities explicit.
