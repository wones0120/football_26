# Observed workload schedule contrasts v1

G21-M05 compares official workload per observed player-week log on short rest (provider rest strictly fewer than seven days) against at least seven days. G21-M07 compares native sheltered roof states (dome/closed) against exposed states (outdoors/open). Each is positive-cohort mean minus negative-cohort mean, separately for official pass attempts, carries and targets. Rest threshold and roof grouping are explicit definitions, not fitted adjustments, causal conclusions or current pregame forecasts.

Each comparison stays within one native actor, canonical attachment, team and season. The strict weekly/schedule period, native identity, unique game/team binding, canonical conflicts, official physical count and unknown exposure boundary is reused from workload_venue_splits. Unknown context masks exact comparisons rather than dropping observations into a complete-case comparison. Unbound native team evidence masks that actor's same-season comparisons. Both cohorts need observed source participation; missing player rows never become zero/DNP. Source position-proven QB scope remains required for passing exposure. No histories cross team/season.

Source is the High-reviewed immutable weekly component and projected raw extract plus the indexed immutable schedule. Original source/import/capture timestamps are retained separately from actual current read/derive/publication. No fresh provider request, DB mutation, alias repair, model fit, scenario activation or optimizer action occurs. All outputs remain revised postlock descriptive and not pregame serving. Native schedule roof may be revised realized-game state; no historical forecast availability is inferred. Provider rest is not independently certified from every historical game completion.

This is two proposed catalog definitions pending High, not six extra IDs from three channels each. Internal artifact only; no UI/configuration behavior changed and no global release claim is made.

## Actual coverage

Authenticated composition produced 7,828 native actor/team/season profiles and 104 SF/WAS 2026 selected profiles. Exact rest comparisons: 163 QB passing, 4,551 rushing and 4,551 target contrasts. Exact roof comparisons: 209 QB passing, 5,472 rushing and 5,472 target contrasts. Others retain unknown/missing-cohort states. Actual composition took 3.74 seconds, with no new source, DB mutations or model operations.
