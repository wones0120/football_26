# Declared scheduled-venue displacement (G20-M10)

Origin is the immediately preceding scheduled REG game venue for the same team and season, including across byes. Destination is the measured game's venue. This describes scheduled venue displacement, not actual team itinerary, flight miles, home-base travel, fatigue or a causal football response. No model or consumer integration.

Native schedule game/team/stadium IDs bind the existing reviewed versioned registry. Exact native-game overrides take precedence: some international rows retain a nominal home stadium on the wrong continent. Neutral/unknown location without a reviewed override, duplicate effective mappings, absent coordinates/timezones, missing kickoff, ambiguous local kickoff, uncertain predecessor or missing season beginning remain unknown. Display names never select venues. Scope is 2024–25 REG only; 17 unique scheduled games per team and 272 native games per season are corroborated, not presumed for arbitrary sources. First games are not applicable, not zero displacement. No offseason origin.

Distance is spherical great-circle kilometers with declared mean Earth radius 6371.0088 km. Signed timezone difference is destination UTC offset minus origin UTC offset, with BOTH IANA zones evaluated at the SAME current kickoff instant. Thus a DST transition at the same venue cannot fabricate travel. Native gametime is Eastern; ambiguous/nonexistent wall time is unknown. Exact TZif bytes are frozen for reproducibility. Historical offset at origin kickoff is not substituted.

The prior WTHR-001 registry review documents native IDs/effective seasons, 37 physical venues and game-specific overrides. Per-record native PFR/official game evidence and Wikidata coordinate URIs are preserved alongside exact registry bytes and the source-controlled pure validation loader. This reuses reviewed definitions; it is not a fresh independent coordinate measurement. The immutable schedule is revised historical provider-returned CSV, not original remote Parquet or proven original-pregame availability. Both clocks and unknown chronology remain explicit. Independent High review must adjudicate actual venue/coordinate/timezone proof before full promotion.

Each team-game measurement is distinct from a unique game. Full-profile distance sums require every applicable displacement known; known-sample sums remain separately named. Unknown values are never filled with zero. No player joins, aliases, source capture, DB mutation, training, optimization or serving.
