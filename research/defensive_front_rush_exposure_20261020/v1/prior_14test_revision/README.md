# Historical defensive personnel and directional rush exposure

Candidate X04-M06 only. G16-M08 front-alignment/assignment or future matchup completion is not claimed. Feature-only work; models and serving remain paused.

The joint exposure is a native official nonkneel, non-two-point, non-nullified rush (including scramble once) with an explicit native offensive/opponent side, left/middle/right run_location and eleven unique source-proven defensive GSIS participants aligned to their reported positions. Personnel tuple: DL={DE,DT,NT}; LB={LB,ILB,OLB,MLB}; DB={CB,S,FS,SS}; all other explicit reported roles are a separate OTHER residual. OLB stays LB. Missing positions and explicit unknown markers prevent certification. These are reported personnel-family counts, never actual front alignment, edge/interior responsibilities, box positions, scheme, protection assignment or designed-rush intent.

Native run_gap is retained separately on every event. It is not inferred from run_location, nor used to infer location. Location-based directional exposure is the declared catalog view; joint gap stratification is an optional later refinement.

Cells retain rush exposure counts and signed same-play source-credited rushing-yard and provider-derived EPA sums/means, with independent matched known-outcome denominators and units. Unknown outcome keeps the exposure known, while only that outcome's exact statistic becomes null. Empty known cells support count/total zero and undefined mean. Unknown eligibility, side, participation, personnel or direction may belong to multiple cells: all affected game's/profile's exact cells are conservatively masked, retaining separate known-exposure lower bounds and known-outcome samples. Unknown-side events are possible side members, not known team rush counts. Expected game sides remain present even when no eligible/classified rush is observed.

The immutable original participation captures and retained PBP manifests must match exact native season/game/play/phase/possession; duplicate keys or contradictory PBP game sides reject. Team-grain aggregation includes every native rusher/defender, with no player-name join, canonical filter or salary/role selection. Participation input is explicitly reviewed historical nonserving material. Full source receipts, original hashes and helper/producer hashes accompany artifacts; actual read/derive/publication clocks are retained.

2023+ participation is published after the postseason. Replayed source and 2026 captures do not establish original pregame availability. No current2026 fallback, forward probability, calibrated matchup, model fit or causal interpretation is supplied. The targeted tests cover source key/side conflicts, exact participant cardinality and cross-side conflict, unsupported-vs-unknown positions, scrambled rush accounting, exclusions, unknown memberships/outcomes, signed values, empty cells and deterministic native-key output.
